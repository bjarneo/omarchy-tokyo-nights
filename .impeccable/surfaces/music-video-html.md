# Music video surface

## Scope

The music video uses Experience mode. The exported MP4 is the primary artifact.
The browser preview plays the same deterministic sequence against the supplied audio.
The user supplies the MP3 and all lyric text.

The social export adapts the completed MP4 to five 9:16 shorts.
Each short retains the source audio and uses a distinct lyric passage.

## Direction contract

### THESIS

The game's yellow Lamborghini travels through the places in the song, from Shinjuku rain to Tokyo Bay sunrise.
The song directs the camera, traffic, weather, and neon accents.

### OWN-WORLD

The video inherits the game's Tokyo Night palette, pixel sprites, skyline, and square arcade lettering.
Pink signs, cyan rain, and gold bodywork remain visible against the indigo city.

### STORY

The opening establishes the city and song title. Verses follow the route and lyrics.
Choruses accelerate through neon and bridge scenes. The bridge enters a tunnel.
The outro parks the car beside the bay as dawn replaces the night.

### FIRST VIEWPORT

A narrow song heading precedes a large 16:9 canvas. A compact transport row sits directly below the canvas.
The opening frame places the title above the skyline and the yellow car below it.
The Play video button provides the primary action. Section links permit precise jumps through the song.

### FORM

The user pins the existing game artwork and a song-led video sequence.
This direct extension uses code-led canvas art. An open-world seed does not apply.
Beat accents affect exhaust and road reflections. Vocal timestamps control the lyric captions and scene cuts.

## Social shorts

The portrait frame places a two-line lyric headline above the video and word-timed captions below it.
The source crop follows the car through each shot. The sunrise short uses a wider crop to retain the car and sun.
Important text stays between 90 and 1700 vertical pixels within the 1080 × 1920 frame.
The colors and Arcade face inherit the music video. The short export uses no new visual identity.
The five passages cover the first chorus, first verse, second chorus, tunnel bridge, and sunrise outro.
